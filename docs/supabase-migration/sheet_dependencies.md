## Takeaways for the Supabase migration
1. **Other systems also WRITE to Revenue:** Revenue Spunky Online (primary writer for the other channels; positional A:AB; inserts/deletes rows), CRM Spunky (appendRow by header), and the Cancellation LINE OA bot (rewrites C–I and T for approved cancellations of LINE Shop orders). The legacy คนรักเอมโอชา can also write if it's still deployed. So Revenue can't simply move to Supabase: those systems keep writing non-LINE-shop orders there.
2. **Other systems also WRITE to Members Emocha Club:** Cancellation LINE OA (Members F/L, Points_Log), Notice Line Privilleges (flag columns in Member_Privileges, Coupons, Signup_Privileges, and adds header columns), and the legacy คนรักเอมโอชา. If Members, Points_Log or privileges move to Supabase, these writes will either go to a stale sheet or diverge.
3. **Read-only consumers:** Line Bot Report (Revenue, filters Ad="line shop" and uses วันที่ชำระเงิน, so LINE Shop orders **must keep landing in Revenue** or the daily report goes empty), Meeting Dashboard (Revenue + Members + privileges + targets), Revenue Spunky Online's dashboards (Revenue AE for LINE Shop; Members A–J lookup), and Stock Flow (MDS "SKU ส่งออก", only at setup).
4. **Positional coupling:** Revenue Spunky Online and the Cancellation bot index Revenue columns by fixed position (A–AE). Members LINE writes by position too. A column insert or reorder in Revenue breaks them. The header-based readers (Line Bot Report, Meeting Dashboard, CRM Spunky) survive column moves but not header renames.
5. **Master Data Sales:** the "SKU" tab is read by Members LINE, the legacy คนรักเอมโอชา, and Revenue Spunky Online (B=name, D=price). The "SKU ส่งออก" tab is read by CRM Spunky and Stock Flow. The only writer is Meeting Dashboard, and only to its own DashboardKeywordGroups_Product tab.
6. Side note: Revenue Spunky Online and คนรักเอมโอชา hard-code LINE channel access tokens in their source, and แสกน QR code does too.
